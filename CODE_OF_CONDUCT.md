# Code of conduct

Treat people who ask questions, submit changes and review your work with respect. Give specific technical feedback. Explain which behavior you observed, how to reproduce it, or which tradeoff you would prefer. Contributors of every background and level of experience are welcome.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks and sharing someone's private information have no place here. Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation to keep project spaces usable.

To report abuse privately, use **Report content** on the relevant GitHub issue, comment or pull request, or contact GitHub Support through its reporting form. [GitHub's reporting guide](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
